\## Overview



This repository contains the dataset pipelines, analytical models, and visualization tools for my capstone project. The research examines the global distribution of artificial intelligence adoption, exploring whether economic wealth ($GDP\\ per\\ capita$) acts as a primary driver for AI integration or if regional policy, infrastructure, and demographic factors play a larger role.



\## Key Research Objectives



\* \*\*Spatial Analysis:\*\* Map global AI usage patterns across low, middle, and high-income nations.

\* \*\*Economic Correlation:\*\* Quantify the relationship between $GDP\\ per\\ capita$ and the rate of generative \& operational AI adoption.

\* \*\*Disparity Identification:\*\* Highlight regions experiencing an "AI adoption gap" relative to their economic output.
